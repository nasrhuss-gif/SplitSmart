# Confirmed lab clarification decisions

Confirmed by the user of this chat on 2026-10-29: D1–D9 as the initial proposed rules, D10 as the login follow-up, and D11–D23 by explicit approval of the complete remaining-decisions proposal. These are subsequent lab decisions, not text from the original customer request. UserRequirement.md remains unchanged.

| Decision | Confirmed behavior | Traceability |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | One payer per expense. The payer may also be a participant. | Q1; FR-003, FR-007, FR-010 |
| D2 | Expense participants must be current group members. | Q2; FR-002, FR-007 |
| D3 | USD with two decimal places. Allocate leftover cents by stable participant order. | Q3; FR-004–005, FR-010–011 |
| D4 | Reject nonpositive or malformed amounts. | Q4; FR-003 |
| D5 | Reject percentage inputs whose total is not 100%. | Q5; FR-005 |
| D6 | Real user login is required. | Q6; FR-001 |
| D7 | Send in-app notifications when expenses are submitted. | Q7; FR-008 |
| D8 | One group-manager approval is required before an expense posts to balances. | Q8; FR-009–010 |
| D9 | Record pairwise settlement payments. Partial payments are allowed; reject overpayments. | Q9; FR-010–011 |
| D10 | Use email/password sign-in with email verification and password recovery. | Q6; FR-001 |
| D11 | Creator and payer may differ. For a group expense, both must be current group members. Exactly one payer funds the full expense; the payer may be excluded from the selected participants. The creator explicitly selects the payer and participants. | Q1 |
| D12 | Any authenticated user may create a group and becomes its manager. Managers add registered users, appoint additional managers, and remove members. Removal requires that member's group balances be zero and no pending expense/payment involves them. Historical records are retained. Direct individual sharing uses a private two-person group, with both users as managers; it follows the same financial rules. Managers cannot access unrelated groups. | Q2 |
| D13 | USD only; reject an amount with more than two decimal places. Use immutable user IDs in ascending order as the stable allocation order. For equal or percentage splits, calculate exact shares with BigDecimal, round each share down to whole cents, then assign one residual cent at a time in stable order until the shares equal the total. No currency conversion. | Q3 |
| D14 | Require a positive expense amount, a valid calendar date in YYYY-MM-DD format, and a nonblank description of at most 500 characters. Accept valid past/current/future dates without inventing a business restriction. Reject missing/malformed fields with a field-specific message and no posting. Negative refunds are unsupported in the lab MVP; correcting an approved expense uses a reviewed reversal and a replacement expense, retaining the original record. | Q4 |
| D15 | Each selected participant's percentage must be greater than zero and at most 100, with at most two decimal places. The exact decimal sum must be 100.00%; there is no tolerance and no automatic normalization. Fractional-cent allocations follow D13. | Q5 |
| D16 | Require email verification before account access. Store salted password hashes and never plaintext passwords. Verification and reset links are single use and expire after 30 minutes. Reset requests return the same generic response for registered and unknown emails. Users can see their own balances and payments and shared expense/receipt information for groups to which they belong; managers have no access to unrelated groups. Retain former members' access to their own historical financial records. Use persistent storage, secure transport for web/mobile communication, and no sensitive credentials in logs or public source control. Exact security implementation is a later engineering decision. | Q6 |
| D17 | Persist an unread in-app notification for every other current group member when an expense is submitted. Creation of the pending expense and notification records is atomic. Repeated delivery with the same notification ID does not duplicate the notice. Users may mark notifications read; read state persists. No email or push notification is required for the lab. | Q7 |
| D18 | A group manager other than the expense creator approves or rejects. If no eligible manager exists, the expense remains pending until another manager is appointed. Pending/rejected expenses have no ledger effect. The creator may edit/cancel a pending expense or revise/resubmit a rejected one; revisions require approval again. Approved expenses cannot be edited/deleted in place; the creator may request a reversal, which another manager must approve. A reversal undoes the original ledger entries once and preserves an audit trail. | Q8 |
| D19 | A debtor records a positive pairwise payment, and the recipient confirms it before balances change. Reject payment above the debt when submitted and again when confirmed. Allow partial settlement. Pending payments may be canceled by the recorder. A confirmed payment can be reversed only after both parties confirm the reversal. Every expense approval, payment confirmation, and reversal has a unique operation ID; repeat submissions have one financial effect. This records real-world payments and does not transfer money. | Q9 |
| D20 | Optional JPEG or PNG receipt images, maximum 5 MiB per expense, one image per expense. Validate file content as well as extension. Access follows the associated expense's group permissions. Retain receipt and financial history for the lifetime of the account/group in this lab; deletion/retention policies for production require a separate decision. | Q10 |
| D21 | Include expense, balance, and payment summaries plus optional inclusive start/end date filters and CSV export. Expense reports use the entered expense calendar date; confirmed payment reports use the confirmation date in UTC. Balances are explicitly labeled current, not historical as-of values. Financial totals include approved expenses and confirmed payments; pending/rejected records may appear separately by status and do not affect totals. Custom arbitrary report builders are deferred. | Q11 |
| D22 | Approve a Java Swing lab MVP covering all core functional capabilities: accounts, groups/private pairs, both split methods, receipts, in-app notifications, manager review, pairwise balances, settlement records, and date-filtered summaries/CSV. Web, Android, and iOS remain full-product requirements delivered in later increments; the desktop MVP is not a substitute. Approved desktop support: Windows 11, macOS 14+, Ubuntu 24.04 LTS with Java 21. Approved later product support: the latest two stable Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari releases, Android 12+, and iOS 17+, with the same core functionality. | Q12 |
| D23 | Approved MVP acceptance benchmark: 100 users, 10 groups, 10,000 expenses on an 8 GB RAM reference machine; 95% of local expense submission, balance, and report operations complete within 2 seconds, measured over 100 representative requests per operation. All interactive desktop controls are keyboard reachable, inputs have accessible labels, and validation is not communicated by color alone. Provide daily backup and a demonstrated restore with recovery point at most 24 hours and recovery time at most 1 hour. No hosted availability SLA applies to the desktop MVP. Approved later web availability target is 99.5% per calendar month. These are targets, not test results. | Q13 |
